About the founder

Who is behind Orbi?

I'm Anna Villebro Rasmussen, and I work on Orbi full time from Denmark.

You should know who is behind the app you write your messages in.

Why I built Orbi

I wanted somewhere to talk with my friends and plan the things we actually do together. The apps I had kept turning into something else along the way: feeds to scroll, things to react to, and a gentle pull to stay a little longer. The practical parts got lost in it — who is coming on Saturday, whose birthday is tomorrow, where we said we would meet.

Orbi is what I wanted instead. It sits in the space between a chat app and social media, and stays firmly on the practical side: messages, events and birthdays, with no advertising, no algorithmic feed, and nobody writing to you out of the blue unless you accept them first.

The other reason is where it comes from. Nearly everything we talk to each other through is built outside Europe, under other rules, and paid for by advertising. I wanted the alternative to exist: a communication tool made here, kept to European privacy standards, and run so that the people using it are the ones it answers to.

What I did before Orbi

I hold a master's degree in Design and Innovation Engineering (cand.polyt.) from the Technical University of Denmark, with an exchange year at Queen Mary University of London, and I have spent my working life designing digital products.

At the National Genome Center I was lead UX designer on tools used in Danish healthcare, where the data people hand over is about as personal as it gets, and where trust has to hold up in the details rather than in the marketing. At the LEGO Group I designed internal digital products together with IT teams and product owners, and learned how software is built, maintained and kept coherent when a great many people depend on it. Orbi puts both of those to work on something I wanted for myself.

How Orbi is run

  • Free, and free of advertising. Orbi does not sell personal data, profiles or access to you, and it does not make product decisions to keep you on the screen longer.
  • Encrypted end to end. Messages and calls are encrypted between the people in them, so I cannot read or listen to them either.
  • Danish and European. Orbi is run from Denmark under Danish and EU law, and its backend is hosted in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Registered in Denmark as [company name], CVR [number].

What Orbi collects, and what it does not, is written out in the privacy policy.
