About the founder

Who is behind Orbi?

I’m Anna Villebro Rasmussen, MSc in Engineering, and I work on Orbi full time from Denmark.

You should know who is behind the app you write your messages in.

Why I built it

I wanted one place to talk with my friends and plan the things we actually do together. The apps I was using kept drifting into something else: feeds to scroll, posts to react to, a quiet nudge to stay a bit longer. The practical parts got buried — who’s coming on Saturday, whose birthday is tomorrow, where we said we’d meet.

So I built what I wanted instead. Orbi sits between a chat app and a social network, and stays on the practical side of that line: messages, events and birthdays. No advertising, no algorithmic feed, and no strangers turning up in your inbox — people can only reach you once you’ve accepted them.

There’s a second reason. Almost everything we talk to each other through is built outside Europe, under someone else’s rules, and paid for by advertising. I wanted the alternative to exist: a communication tool made here, held to European privacy standards, and answerable to the people using it.

What I did before

I have a master’s in Design and Innovation Engineering (cand.polyt.) from the Technical University of Denmark, including an exchange year at Queen Mary University of London, and I’ve spent my working life designing digital products.

At the National Genome Center I was working as the UX designer on tools used in Danish healthcare, where the data people hand over is about as personal as it gets, and trust comes from how the thing actually works, not from what you say about it. At the LEGO Group I designed internal products together with IT teams and product owners, and learned how software gets built, maintained and kept coherent when a lot of people depend on it. Orbi draws on both: the care that personal data demands, and the discipline of building something meant to last.

How Orbi is run

  • Free, and free of advertising. Orbi doesn’t sell personal data, profiles or access to you, and no decision here is made to keep you on the screen longer.
  • Encrypted end to end. Messages and calls are encrypted between the people in them, so I can’t read or hear them either.
  • Danish and European. Run from Denmark under Danish and EU law, with the backend hosted in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Registered in Denmark as [company name], CVR [number].

What Orbi collects, and what it doesn’t, is spelled out in the privacy policy.
